[PCMT NA Season 3](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na) Playoffs come to a close as [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) continues their dominant form with a 3-0 [Grand Finals](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-spg-s3-playoffs-2026-10-28-2) Sweep against [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1).

This series saw [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) coming in as Omega’s #2 seed, previously beating [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) in the upper bracket final to secure a double-map ban advantage, whilst looking in incredibly hot form. [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1), on the other hand, had just beaten NAVI days prior, with [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) dropping 101 kills in a 3-0 sweep, looking to continue in his indomitable form.

[SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) opened map bans by banning Haven and Split. Haven was [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s most dominant map in the tournament (almost in the history of the tournament!) having a 6-0 record on the map, and Split was played in the upper finals where [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) was able to take a pretty convincing 13-9 over [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming).

This leads us to Map 1, where [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) picked [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-spg-s3-playoffs-2026-10-28-2?map=Abyss), [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s permaban, since the map came back into rotation. From agent select, we saw [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) stick to a more standard Jett/Yoru variation of [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-spg-s3-playoffs-2026-10-28-2?map=Abyss), whilst [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) threw a wrench into their team synergy, sticking [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) onto Yoru (the first time he has been off Phoenix in over a month!), and placing [Radical](https://pcmt.gg/players/radical) on Waylay and playing Brimstone on [RoRo](https://pcmt.gg/players/roro).

Needless to say, the agent select was the most entertaining part about [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) on this map. The pistol round was very close, with [dunkn](https://pcmt.gg/players/dunkn) having a 2k after a flank down mid to put the round into a 1v2, and [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) having 3 on the round but not able to close it out. From there, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) was not able to hold a bombsite properly, going down early 4-0 off some nice shots from [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy).

[T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) were able to gain two rounds in a row, as the constant B aggression finally started to overwhelm [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) slightly, but if there's anything we learned about [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming), it's that they know how to bounce back, and they proved that past round 6. It was domination in its purest form from [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming), as they only lost one more round for the rest of the half, based on pure consistency from [dunkn](https://pcmt.gg/players/dunkn) and [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) (10/4/5 and 14/6/5 respectively), ending with a score of 9-3.

Attack was no different story for the spankers. A very quick pistol and bonus conversion put them at an 11-3 lead, and with a 3k from [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) and a 2k from [thighs](https://pcmt.gg/players/thighs), this map ended 13-3 [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming).

Map 2 saw us taking our sights to the [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-spg-s3-playoffs-2026-10-28-2?map=Summit), where [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) resorted back to the classic comp of Phoenix/Cypher/Fade/Omen. [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) swapped roles a bit, with [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) going from Yoru to Viper, leaving the agent in good hands with [derpina](https://pcmt.gg/players/derpina).

This map started relatively the same, as [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) lost pistol and lost the force the next round, giving [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) an easy 3k on round 3 to put [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) up 3-0. From here, the momentum kept rolling for [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming). A 3k from [derpina](https://pcmt.gg/players/derpina), and SEVERAL 3k’s from [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) saw [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) start this game 9-0, with [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) headlining at a 15-5 scoreline at this point.

It only took 9 rounds, but [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) finally showed up, blessing [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) with a 4k and a 3k to close the game 9-3 once again. Once again, [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) won another pistol and bonus, but [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) were finally able to convert on a bonus and showed some slight promise, but the bullet train of [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) was unstoppable, pushing past this map in a very brief 13-5, once again highlighted by [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) and [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) at the top of the scoreboard.

Down 2-0, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) needed to pull out every stop they had if they wanted to continue this season, and [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-spg-s3-playoffs-2026-10-28-2?map=Lotus) was the demanding map to do so. This is where [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) plays the iconic [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) chamber, where they first broke out onto the scene, with [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) running the same comp as usual.

The nice thing about this map was that this was a show from the start. [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) was able to finally convert a pistol, and with some very well-called rounds from both sides, we saw a 2-1 scoreline after the first three rounds.

Straight consistency in post plants from [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) saw them winning 3 straight, boasting a 4-2 scoreline. [Radical](https://pcmt.gg/players/radical) was becoming [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s saving grace, as back-to-back 3Ks and 4s saw [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) close the distance, but Thighs goes uncleared in a corner for a huge 4 K the next round, as highlights kept spiraling out of control. The half ended 7-5 in [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming)’s favor, headlined by [Radical](https://pcmt.gg/players/radical)s (10/7/0) and [thighs](https://pcmt.gg/players/thighs) & Cooleo (14/6/1, 14/7/1).

[MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) ended the half at a 10/10 scoreline, but exploded out of the gates with a 4k in the pistol to secure their 2nd of the series. A bonus conversion and a very well-placed eco (including another [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) ult) set the score at 8-7, as the [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) defense was working very well thanks to [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) being placed in B main, as his audio comms were incredible, and the constant Odin 2Ks provided a thorn in the side, but [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) were not affected whatsoever, as [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo)’s consistency provided [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) with an 11-8 lead going into a [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) eco. On this round, [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo) attempted a 1v4 and almost succeeded, but [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) was there to put an end to the hope.
-# Interesting note: [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) actually dropped the Odin after this round and started playing Stellar with the rifle!


After this round, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) mounted a comeback to make the score 11-11, and neither team was able to push the final hurdle, as straight trades and an eco win from [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) pushed this game into overtime.

In overtime, we started with a near-flawless round from [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming), turned into back to back flawless rounds from [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1), seeing the score go 14-13 in [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s favor. An aptly C postplant evened the score, and [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) capitalized while the lights were the absolute brightest. A converted C postplant and early picks on round 30 sent [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) into a 5v3 that they could not squander, as they converted it to win 16 - 14, crowning [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) as the finals winners and champions of PCMT NA S3.

This game was highlighted by [phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy)’s (54/35/17), [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman)’s (60/49/12), and our **FINALS MVP**, [cooleo](https://pcmt.gg/players/cooleo), going (65/39/16). That’s all for season 3; we hope to see you in season 4 :)
