PCMT NA SEASON 4 HAS BEGUN!!

A bit sooner than expected, but we have matches on the slate, this one being no different! We see the return of the Wingman Warriors core with a couple of new faces, as [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) takes on [Lucas](https://pcmt.gg/players/lucas) & [Igneous](https://pcmt.gg/players/igneous)’ [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming). This is the first match of PCMT NA Season 4, taking place in Group A.

SUBS: Caesparz for Sylos. ([FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz))

This game started with [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming)’s pick of [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/fnc-vs-wbg-s4-group-a-2026-11-18?map=Lotus). [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) saw a bit of a swap in roles, as Psyclone took on the omen, with [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) being put on Jett. [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) saw a slight role swap: [Lucas](https://pcmt.gg/players/lucas) played Hush, and [Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) played Vyse.

The pistol round started with a classic C exec, where [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) were able to overpower [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) starting up with the pistol and the converted bonus. [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) rang true on round 3, finding a 3k in water to bounce back the next round, and the double LMG play from [Igneous](https://pcmt.gg/players/igneous) and [Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) was too much to handle, making the score 2-2. 

The next couple of rounds turned even, as we even saw [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) hit a 1 hp defuse clutch on the C site to make it 3-3. The next couple of rounds saw some fast execs from [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz), a classic staple of [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) teams, and even with a [Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) 4k, it was too much for [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) to handle, going down 3-7. A [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) 3k was able to close out one more round, but from here it was downhill for [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming). A [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) lurk into A secures him a 3k going into the half, and the [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) attack was dreadful.

We saw [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) win the pistol and [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) force the next round, only for it to have no legs. [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) rode this momentum to an 11-4, lead, and there was no looking back, as [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) kept on clapping, and with the solid foundation of the rest of [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz), they took the map 13-4, highlighted by [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) (25/10/5), and [radical](https://pcmt.gg/players/radical) (19/8/4)

Our second map saw us taking our talents to the snowy hills of [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/fnc-vs-wbg-s4-group-a-2026-11-18?map=Corrode), where [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) switched it up and opted for a double senti comp, with [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) on chamber and [Lucas](https://pcmt.gg/players/lucas) on vyse. ([Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) neon!!! omg!!!1!!!!), while getting more of the same comp from [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz).

[WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) got off to a much hotter start, with voodood getting a 3k on the pistol round to secure a [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) pistol win. After a clean bonus conversion thanks to [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel)s Outlaw, a [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) default with a [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) lurk 3k turned the score 2-1. [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) were not deterred though, as a [Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) on the off role neon started to shine, getting a 4k with a rifle and a judge to secure more rounds for [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming), while [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) still struggled to find their footing on the attack against the double senti comp. Another 4k from [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) secured this ideal, as we looked at a 6-2 scoreline for the gamers going into an [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) timeout.

Off the timeout, [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) did a fast B pop that worked wonders, with [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) coming online with a 3k, and deciding to do the same thing the next round as well. Once again we saw a multikill from the [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel)/[Caleb](https://pcmt.gg/players/caleb) duo, as [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) were able to one of the last two, as [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) were able to orchestrate a beautiful post plant on B with the nameless sova ult to scure the last round, ending the half 7-5.

Going into the half, we wanted to see how [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming)’s double senti comp would do on corrode attack, and we were met with a very swift pistol and eco win from [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz). What unfolded after this is nothing short of tragic for [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming), as the next 6/7 (haha) rounds were pure domination from [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz). Constant [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) 3k’s with an op, [Caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) anchoring efficiently; [Lucas](https://pcmt.gg/players/lucas) and [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) were trying their damnedest to get something going, but every time they caught a lifeline, [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) would punch back with one twice as hard. The score of this map ended 13-8, with [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) taking the series 2-0.

Once again, highlighted by [Claps](https://pcmt.gg/players/claps) (56/20/10) and [Pavel](https://pcmt.gg/players/pavel) (40/29/8), this puts [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) in the 1-0 bracket and [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) into the 0-1 bracket.
