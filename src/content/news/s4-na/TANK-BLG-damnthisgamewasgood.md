This is the first matchup of Group D, seeing [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)ers ([TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)) with [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus), [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg), [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus), [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum), and [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee), go against Bartho Lomew Gaming ([BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming)) with [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting), [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara), [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher), [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz), and [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy). This matchup is not only equivalent on paper (highest rank is Ascendant 2, lowest is Plat 1!), but we also see 8/10 members from the NAVI 5 stack pitted against each other, with a fated matchup of [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) & [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) against [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting).

Our map 1 takes us to [Haven](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/blg-vs-tank-s4-group-d-2026-11-23?map=Haven), where we get to see [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming)’s roles unfold for the first time. [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) (normally init/smokes/chamber), instalocks the Hush as he claims to be on his hush arc for now. We see everyone else play common roles, filling out the team cleanly, with [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) on sova, [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara) on Omen, [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) on cypher, and [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) on Phoenix. On the side of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers), we are treated with more [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) phoenix (fuck) for Haven, while newcomer [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) slots onto the chamber. The roles from there fill out nicely with [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) on the sova, [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) on omen, and [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) taking charge on neon.

Our pistol round starts with [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) on the attacking side, bu[Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) the gates with a fast mid take, challenged by all of [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) (minus [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz)). This attempt is quickly thwarted, as [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) win the trades 3-1 and takes the pistol as well as the bonus very convincingly. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) came back on the next round, just picking off the [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) players individually, winning the 1v1 game. A very scrappy round 4 ensued, with [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) getting the first blood but [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) almost instantly trading 2 right back. The trades ended in the favor of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers), which spiraled them to a 3-1 lead. The next round was a same affair, this time setting up in a post plant 3v4, [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) used a sova ult and got a 3k, but [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) was able to close out the round to keep [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) in this game.

After this conversion, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) stumbled slightly to the force of [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) (1k) and [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus)’s (3k!) ultimates, forcing them onto eco the next round. [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) was able to convert this round, very narrowly, giving them a 5-2 lead. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) then roared back with a round thanks to [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) and [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) providing excellent aggression and Anchoring on the A site. We then got our mandatory [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) round, where a 3k settled the deal. Once again knocked onto an eco, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) take a round loss and move into a buy round, where [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) gets put into a 1v2 against [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) and [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum). Fortunately for them, [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) made a mis-step, and they very cleanly isolated both fights and won the round, which gave them enough momentum to close out the half to make it a 7-5 defense side Haven.

For defense, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) decide to pick up right where they left off; aggression. They commit to a triple walkdown mid that does not work in their favor, being put into a 4v2 retake that [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) easily tackle, as well as the anti-eco round. Round 15 saw a phoenix lurk up onto C, where we saw some incredible Hush tech with [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) and [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting). [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) pulled [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) into a swing, killing the chamber on plat, and basically guaranteeing the bonus for [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming). On the back heel, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) saved their weapons and their pride, and contacted around the map to secure a round from pure firepower. Down 9-8, [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) rips his sova ult off of pressure A main and the round starts as a 5v3 for [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers). Unfortunately, [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) has the ability to do anything, and somehow brings this round to a 2v2 after killing both of the C anchors. This does not amass to anything, as [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) gets caught out on a lurk mid and [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) convert easily. 

At a tied 9-9, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) lowk just get pissed off and hard exec C and win. Valid when playing against Chamber. Off this round, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) shifts Chamber to A and again contacts for an OP kill, and at the end of the trades, [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara) is left in a 1v2 that they barely can't convert against [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus). Once again, we see [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) take advantage of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)’s weak C site, targeting it two rounds in a row and, off of some very clean [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) kills, are able to hit match point first at a 12-10 scoreline. Unfortunately for [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming), [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) swallow their pride and win back-to-back rounds off of well-timed and placed fights, including a very clinical C retake with the phoenix ult from [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus).

To start overtime, we see [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) opening with an immediate 2k on A long to put [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) in a 4v3 retake scenario. Unfortunately, [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) decides his consistency isn't enough and explodes through the round with a 4k, willing to take his team the distance. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) pick up where they left off, pressuring the huge gap that is [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)’s C site, and pull through a very close round, including a huge 2k from [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz). At 13-13, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) are shut down on the C split attempt, and although [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) hits an insane 3k, it's not enough to stop [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) and [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) from closing out the round. With [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) on match point, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) kicks up the gears and completely dismantles any attempt at winning this round, winning a flawless round to set the score at 14-14. This high gear stays in place, as within 5 seconds of the round starting, they put themselves in a 5v2. In any normal scenario, this is a round win. Unfortunately, [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) is a monster with so many variables that you cannot account for what he will do.

[TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) starts the B exec, and [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) swings out of the A link smoke, instantly killing [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) and [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg). He pushes into backsite, and by grabbing the A main orb earlier, activates the Suspension, which catches Androbee on top B. [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) tries to catch [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) off guard by taking a timing in C link, but with [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) in flow state, he dismantles the entire team by himself and lets [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) deal with the last remaining player to put [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) up 15-14.

Now, this would've been a good point to end the map, but it seems when [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) is pushed down, they push back up even harder. [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) pushes up A main and gets 2 kills (and the bomb!) that give [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) enough info to convert the defense round. Sitting at a 15-15 scoreline, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) contact into a C take, where once again we see [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) kick into high gear (literally and figuratively), as he secures a 3k and the easy attacker round. Once again, though, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) punch back with a contact A pop into an OT flawless of their own, setting the score of this game at 16-16.

This was about the time of the game that [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) had enough. He single-handedly got into the A site, killing not only all the site players but also the flanker and the retaker in heaven, giving [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) match point. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) went to their tried-and-true C exec, but unfortunately [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) made the correct adjustments. [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) finds a pick with the OP on C long, [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) pushes out mid to kill the lurk, and this game ends at a 18-16 scoreline in favor of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers). This map was highlighted by the dynamic duo of [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) (31/24/13) and [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) (30/26/10), while [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) (29/25/8) was able to hold his own against this force. Also, shout-out to [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) for getting 23 assists. (19/23/23)

That was just map 1, btw! Both teams' first maps! For map 2, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) and [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) are thrown back into the ring in [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/blg-vs-tank-s4-group-d-2026-11-23?map=Summit). This time we got to see [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) run [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara) on the sage, and we got to see our 3rd ever miks with [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) picking up the agent! This was rounded out by [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) on chamber, [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) on fade, and [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting) back on the hush. For [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers), we saw [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) and [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) on the same agents, with [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) picking up the fade, [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) on the Omen (thank fuck), and [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) on the Hush.

[BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) came out the gates with a chip on their shoulder, feeling like they should've won the first map. A 3k on pistol from [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher), and another 3k on the bonus from [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) started this snowball of momentum that led them to start their defense side up 5-0. I would annotate more, but [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) seriously was just getting shit on.

After the 5-0 start, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) actually started playing the game. A slow default into a B execute still caused them to be on their back legs, but [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) was able to shift the momentum with a 3k and a 1v1 clutch. This enabled them to win another round of a fairly unstable econ, after a B exec was shifted into a free A site. After [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) got a 3k, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) felt something was wrong and took a timeout to shift the formation. Despite the [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) first pick, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) were not able to hold their own against the [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) retake, and with another round gone, this put them down 7-2. [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) called a timeout of their own, and then something shifted. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) won on [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)’s eco round fairly convincingly, but at this 8-2 scoreline, something snapped inside of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers)’s brain. A pacy contact play into B site gave them a free site and the postplant victory, and the next round saw them with an early first blood from [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) that turned into a 4k to end the half at an 8-4 scoreline. 

On the defense side, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) did a mid push that granted [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) a 3k but at a cost, as both of the players left to retake the lost A site in a 2v2 were both 1 hp, and were dismantled easily by [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting)’s bandit. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) won the pistol, but [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) had a nice response, as when [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) tried a pacy A take, a very well-placed displacement (nice) from [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) was able to align [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara) and [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/sting), and [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) would convert on this man advantage with another 3 kills from [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus). Duo Ace! From here, the snowball effectively started and didn't stop for [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers). They were able to beat the weaker weapons from [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming), and with a utility to deal with this OP, [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) positioned himself for another 3k/4k round. 


[Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) was dismantling them from the left, [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) was oping them from the right, and it felt like they couldn’t get their foot down as the score went from 8-4 to a very sudden 9-8, soon to be converted to 9-9 thanks to a [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) Op 4k of his own. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) called their 2nd and last timeout of the game, which they were able to use effectively on a mid default into a fast B pop, where they converted their 2nd attack round of the game.

After this round, [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) wanted to test [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) (the kid) and see what he could do. [androobee](https://pcmt.gg/players/androobee) settled with a 2k, but [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) fired back hard and forced [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) into a 1v2 situation. Somehow (and I mean somehow) [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) was able to isolate both players and, with a very well-timed teleport, secured the victory, putting the game at an even 10-10 scoreline. The next round was a very well traded round from both sides that landed in favor of [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers), and with the 11th round on the board, [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) took the fight directly into [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) in main, and with [Angus](https://pcmt.gg/players/Angus) getting a 2k off rip, the execute was dismantled before it even started. [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) tried to mount some sort of momentum, but on the 12-10 match point, we got our mandatory [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg) round, where he exploded for a 4k and secured the 2-0 for [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers), winning [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/blg-vs-tank-s4-group-d-2026-11-23?map=Summit) 13-10. This was highlighted by [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) (23/12/13), and all sentinels [Phuzzy](https://pcmt.gg/players/phuzzy) (22/18/3), [Amara](https://pcmt.gg/players/amara) (20/18/8), and [Ahsum](https://pcmt.gg/players/ahsum) (20/16/0)

When questioned about [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus)’ performance, [Asher](https://pcmt.gg/players/asher) said: “Life Game”. The Media agrees.

This sends [TANK](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/tankers) into the 1-0 bracket in Group D, and sends [BLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/bartho-lomew-gaming) to the 0-1 bracket in the same group. These teams will play the winners/losers of [GENG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/generally-goofy) vs [XLG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/xtremely-lacking-gamesense).
