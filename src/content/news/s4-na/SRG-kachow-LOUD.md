[LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) and [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) both take the stage for this Group A matchup to determine who plays against [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) and who plays against [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz).

For [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), we see [avi](https://pcmt.gg/players/avi), [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino), [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku), and [RoRo](https://pcmt.gg/players/roro) all returning from S3. [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) gears up for a comeback, not being satisfied with his 2nd-place finish in S2. On the side of [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good), we have [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt), [Platinum](https://pcmt.gg/players/platinum), and [paul](https://pcmt.gg/players/paul) all returning for another shot at glory. [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) brings along his two friends, [pieta](https://pcmt.gg/players/pieta) and [Pumpkin](https://pcmt.gg/players/pumpkin), to try and haul their way towards the finish line. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) was favored in this matchup, 56/44 by predictions on fantasy.pcmt.gg (PLUG BOOM!)

For our first map, we took our eyes to the beautiful sunset, where we saw two different approaches from both sides. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) stuck to playing pure comfort, as [RoRo](https://pcmt.gg/players/roro) was seen on the breach, running double initiator with [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) on that fade. [avi](https://pcmt.gg/players/avi) on hush, [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino) on Omen, and [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) on Chamber rounded out the roster. For [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good), we saw a double dive comp with [paul](https://pcmt.gg/players/paul) on Neon and [pieta](https://pcmt.gg/players/pieta) on Hush. This was rounded out by [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) on Chamber, [Platinum](https://pcmt.gg/players/platinum) on Fade, and [Pumpkin](https://pcmt.gg/players/pumpkin) on Astra.

Unfortunately, the pistol round is not live to the world, and I don't remember what happened, so we will start our story at a 2-0 scoreline that's in favor of [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good). [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), with full guns, are able to halt further aggression in A main from this [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) roster, converting a bonus, but not without losing 3 members and dampening the economy a bit. Round 4, we saw a 4-1 split from [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) right onto the B site, where early kills from [pieta](https://pcmt.gg/players/pieta) and [paul](https://pcmt.gg/players/paul) put this in a 3v4 retake scenario. [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino) ended up huge, getting a 2k from market to get something going, but [Pumpkin](https://pcmt.gg/players/pumpkin)’s Astra utility was too much to come over and secured the round for [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good).

The economy was too damaged for the next round, so [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) ran over [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) entirely, and then they did it again. [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) was calling out [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) for not putting the pressure on these executes, and it was working, as in the first 6 rounds they were sitting at a 5-1 scoreline. [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) tested their luck a little too much, but the song was muted by the force of [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) as a contact play into A was shut down by everyone getting a kill. 

[LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) called a timeout after this to discuss how to maybe dampen the aggression, and they geared up for an A fight that never came, because all 5 players on [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) ran down B main and took the site for free (again). [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino) was forced into a 1v3, turned 1v1 but was not able to convert. The next round, despite a 2k to open the round, in a 5v3 with weaker weapons, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) isolated the fights beautifully to set the scoreline at 7-2. Then, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) does what [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) does best, and they ran it down B and were faced with no pressure, and converted ANOTHER B post-plant. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), looking for any lifelines, tried to get aggressive on the A main fight, but [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) were feeling it too much, and they hoisted themselves up 2 more rounds to 9-2. The last round of the half, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) executed B, but this time [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) had ultimates to retake the site with, so thankfully there was some life to come out of the half, as 9-3 curses can be real!

The second half starts with a heavy mid default from [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), turned into a full A exec. [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) is able to garner a 3K, and [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino) closes out a 1v1 with ease, and they then convert the bonus round as well. Unfortunately, the A contact doesn't work the next round, so the scoreline sits at 5-10 for [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion). They decide to test [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good)’s A hold, where they run into 3 players and it doesn't even matter. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) dismantles the entire hold, giving themselves a 6th round and shutting out the defense. [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) bounce back, as a market split goes haywire from the [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) side and [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) convert easily. At 11-6, a cheeky pushdown mid from [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) puts [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) on their back heels, but they push themselves out of that deficit and are able to convert from a 3v4, giving themselves a 7th round. They even fast exec B, and with 3 ultimates to deal with, [RoRo](https://pcmt.gg/players/roro) comes up huge by killing 3 people from the close corner in default, putting this at a very manageable 8-11 scoreline.

Unfortunately, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) called a timeout, and this killed almost all of the momentum that [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) had. They tried to contact into A, but an incredible lurk timing from [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) allowed him to convert 2 backshots, and [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) was not able to hold it down on his lonesome, and this round spiraled into [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) converting the next, giving them the map at a 13-8 scoreline. 

This map was highlighted by incredible performances from [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) (26/11/5), [avi](https://pcmt.gg/players/avi) (22/17/3), and [pieta](https://pcmt.gg/players/pieta) (19/18/11).

With that being [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good)’s map pick, [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) turned their back to the loss and focused on their selection of [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/loud-vs-srg-s4-group-a-2026-11-26?map=Abyss). We saw almost everyone on [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) play the same roles, as [avi](https://pcmt.gg/players/avi), [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku), and [RoRo](https://pcmt.gg/players/roro) were all on the same agents. (breach!!!), with [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) swapping to Sova and [dino](https://pcmt.gg/players/dino) swapping to Astra. On the side of [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good), everyone played the same agent except for [Platinum](https://pcmt.gg/players/platinum) moving to Sova.

Round 1 saw [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) get off on a much better footing, as a fast B execute from [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) was quickly thwarted in the post-plant as aggression into CT left them all dead, with no kills. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) converted the eco flawlessly, and [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) were able to take the bonus after that, setting the score at 2-1. [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) busted out an early OP round 4, and by finding a kill early mid, that set [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) up for a late A execute, which was dismantled by a single recon dart from [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable).

With the score 3-1, [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) once again opens the round up perfectly with an OP kill, and this round is completely shut out from the get-go with a [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) and [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) duo ace. To gain some momentum, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) used [Pumpkin](https://pcmt.gg/players/pumpkin)'s Astra Wall to get a free plant on bridge and delay [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) so much that, despite losing all of their players (with another [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) 3k!), they were unable to convert the round, giving them a lifeline for the 4-2 scoreline.

They took this round and ran with it. The next round was a fast B exec that wasn’t even close. The round after that, [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) managed to halt the momentum, as a stuffed B execute turned into an A pivot that was shut down by [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable), who at this point was (12/2/5). At this 5-3 scoreline, an A execute was trickled down into a 2v2, where [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) and [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) tried to take a double fight into CT, but with no kill conversion. This sets up a beautiful lurk timing into heaven from [Pumpkin](https://pcmt.gg/players/pumpkin), which enables him to not only get a kill on [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable), but pivot the bomb onto the B site, forcing [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) to save, as [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) once again closes the gap 5-4.

The round after that, [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) used all of their ults to convert an A execute, then execute B and convert it, then try to execute A, but [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) was mid to completely bail them out, giving them a streak of 3 to end the [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/loud-vs-srg-s4-group-a-2026-11-26?map=Abyss) attack 7-5.

We all know how attack-sided [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/loud-vs-srg-s4-group-a-2026-11-26?map=Abyss) is, so for [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) it wasn’t looking so bad. The pistol round had other ideas, though, because very well-placed positions from the A players stuffed the split from [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), giving them the pistol. [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) then had a semi-melons round, as [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) and [avi](https://pcmt.gg/players/avi) both hit nice shots on the B-side players, and the Sova recon dart scanned two out of 3 remaining players on A. With this information, [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) fully tried to execute A, which granted [Platinum](https://pcmt.gg/players/platinum) an easy 4k conversion against the weaker weapons in the 5v3.

It seemed like some frustration was settling in for [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion), as a fast A exec crossed them over the line, but not without giving [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) an exit 3k and just swinging to swing. The next round was another fast B take, where on the retake [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) does god’s work from dice, securing a 4v2 scenario for the team. Unfortunately, some very poor positioning from his teammates and some really nice shots from [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) allowed [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) to convert this round, pushing them to double digits at 10-6.

Once again, [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) found two early picks from [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable)s Hunters Fury, and killing the lurker as well gave them a pivot to the B site. Unfortunately, both remaining [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) players had already rotated and were able to isolate every fight completely untraded, giving them their 11th round on [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion)’s force buy.

[SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) were knocking at the door, trying to close this game out, but [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) were not budging just yet. [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) and [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) combined again to convert a post plant after a B exec, giving [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) a lifeline that they needed to close this gap at 7-11. This momentum allowed them to convert a very similar round, pushing their score to 8, but a contact into a stacked B site after enabled [pieta](https://pcmt.gg/players/pieta) for an easy 3K, putting [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) on match and series point. 

From here, there is no magical ending, as [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) take a lot of individual duels that do not go their way, and [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/loud-vs-srg-s4-group-a-2026-11-26?map=Abyss) ends with another 13-8 scoreline in favor of [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good), winning the series 2-0. This map was highlighted by [COLT](https://pcmt.gg/players/colt) (27/14/1), and the dynamic duo of [Sizeable](https://pcmt.gg/players/sizeable) (25/12/7) & [tenyaku](https://pcmt.gg/players/tenyaku) (17/15/5).

This pushes [SRG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/shaymins-really-good) into the upper bracket to face [FNC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/freaks-need-caesparz) in the 1-0 match, while [LOUD](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/leading-ourselves-under-delusion) are pushed down into the lower bracket to face [WBG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/we-been-gaming) in the 0-1 match.
