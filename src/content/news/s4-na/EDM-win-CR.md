The second match to kick off S4 for NA PCMT, we had two teams that have two different preseason expectations. [Everyone Dance Meow](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow), also known as [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) is one of the best teams according to several preseason power rankings. According to the official Bronze Babble preseason power rankings, [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) is THE team to beat, and it’s not hard to see why. [James](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) is consistently one of the most feared players in the whole tournament, and the rest of the roster, consisting of [jaypee](https://pcmt.gg/players/jaypee), [Li](https://pcmt.gg/players/li), [hascan](https://pcmt.gg/players/hascan), and [corina](https://pcmt.gg/players/corina) make up a well-rounded group the rest of the tournament fears. [Crazy Zigzagoon](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon), on the other hand, was the top of C tier in the preseason power rankings. However, the team has a lot of upside, with the return of [jooah](https://pcmt.gg/players/jooah) (of S2 DRG fame). [jooah](https://pcmt.gg/players/jooah) is teamed up with [nolan](https://pcmt.gg/players/nolan), [ceiliuir](https://pcmt.gg/players/ceiliuir), the rookie [nut](https://pcmt.gg/players/nut), and [apple](https://pcmt.gg/players/apple), who is looking for some redemption after being on [EPY](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-entropy) in S3. 

Map one of the series started on [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/edm-vs-cr-s4-group-b-2026-11-20?map=Corrode), with a snag on the preseason top team in terms of their roster. [corina](https://pcmt.gg/players/corina) was not able to make it for map one, so known super sub [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) (S4 BLG) filled the slot until [corina](https://pcmt.gg/players/corina) could make it back for map 2. The quick roster change proved to be too much initially for [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow), as [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon) was able to take a commanding 12-6 lead. However, [caesparz](https://pcmt.gg/players/caesparz) proved to be the person to help [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) bounce back, helping keep the positive vibes for [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) to help push [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) to win 6 rounds in a row to send the first map to overtime. After some back and forth with neither team being able to win a defense round in OT, [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) eventually pulled through, completing the comeback with a 10-2 round run, winning map 1 16-14.

[jaypee](https://pcmt.gg/players/jaypee) proved to be the help that [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) needed to make that comeback, finishing map one with a 29/21/7 scoreline. [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) helped push [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) over the end, with 6 first bloods and 27 kills to finish second on the leaderboard for [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow). [hascan](https://pcmt.gg/players/hascan), however, was the X factor for [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow), as the lowest ranked player on [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) was able to drop 26 kills and get 3 first bloods of his own to help push [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) to win on [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon)’s map pick. On the other side of the leaderboard, [nolan](https://pcmt.gg/players/nolan) finished with a triple double, a 26/24/14 scoreline, to lead all of [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon). [jooah](https://pcmt.gg/players/jooah) and [apple](https://pcmt.gg/players/apple) had very similar performances, both dropping 22 and 20 kills, respectively. Rookie [nut](https://pcmt.gg/players/nut) also had a great map one, finishing with 20 kills as well. 

[corina](https://pcmt.gg/players/corina) came back for [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) in map 2 on their map pick of [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/edm-vs-cr-s4-group-b-2026-11-20?map=Lotus), and it proved to be a map that they practiced a lot in the preseason. [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) had a much different start, starting 7-2 and never looking back. [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) picked up the confident win over [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon) on [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/edm-vs-cr-s4-group-b-2026-11-20?map=Lotus), winning 13-6, with [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) showing why they are consistently revered as one of the top players in all of PCMT. In the 19th round, [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) dropped a 29/12/11 triple-double. [jaypee](https://pcmt.gg/players/jaypee), however, proved to be the Robin to [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james)’ Batman, also dropping a 27/13/4 scoreline, a combined 56 kills between the two players. 

[EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) showed why they are one of the top teams in the tournament, as well as seeing a potential duo of the season with [jaypee](https://pcmt.gg/players/jaypee) and [james](https://pcmt.gg/players/james) dropping 112 kills in the 2 games as a duo. [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon) also showed that they are not a team to be taken lightly, as just one of those rounds flipping on [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/edm-vs-cr-s4-group-b-2026-11-20?map=Corrode) and they would’ve taken a map and possibly flipped around the entire momentum of the series. We’ll see where these two teams end up after the group stages, with [EDM](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/everybody-dance-meow) waiting on the winner and [CR](https://pcmt.gg/events/s4-na/teams/crazy-zigzagoon) waiting on the loser of DEI vs EDG.
