The second round of the lower bracket in [PCMT EMEA Season 2](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea) had a blowout finish that had a [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) victory against [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol), 2-1.

This affair started on [Ascent](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-11-01?map=Ascent), with a very well-executed B-split on pistol, sending [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) 2-0 to start. The fast tempo from [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) was enough to keep overwhelming [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol), but a 3k from [King](https://pcmt.gg/players/king) and [Haike](https://pcmt.gg/players/haike) nabbed [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) their first round, setting the score to be 4-1. From here on out, it was a pure [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) masterclass. NOOT was not able to get any round going, as [Ahmed](https://pcmt.gg/players/hmmmdead)'s chamber & outlaw combination was deadly, and [Ghrf6](https://pcmt.gg/players/ghrf6) starting 17-1 on the Sage, we saw [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) up 9-1 on [Ascent](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-11-01?map=Ascent) attack. [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) were able to claw back 2 rounds at the end of the half, seeing [King](https://pcmt.gg/players/king) finally online with the OP, and a [Jamit](https://pcmt.gg/players/jamit) 4k sending the half over 9-3.

Just as [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) were gaining momentum, [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally)'s super sub of [Nana](https://pcmt.gg/players/nana) stepped in with a 3k on the pistol round, killing any hope [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) had. Despite the best efforts to try to create another 11-1 comeback, [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) would only be able to secure 2 more rounds thanks to a [Taylor](https://pcmt.gg/players/taylorgzz) 3k and 4k, but [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) eventually came over the line 13-5, with [Ghrf6](https://pcmt.gg/players/ghrf6) ending 22/8/3, and [Nana](https://pcmt.gg/players/nana) ending 17/6/11 respectively.

[SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) looked to carry this momentum onto map 2 of [Haven](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-11-01?map=Haven). [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) had other plans, as they wanted to meet [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally)'s aggression head-on, starting with a pistol walk down mid. This fray ended in [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol )'s favor, as [Ghrf6](https://pcmt.gg/players/ghrf6) was not able to clutch the 3v1, and with a bonus conversion, they started up 2-0.

The game seemed to start slipping through, as [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) were able to capitalize on 4 rounds in a row off of some patented [0TAKA](https://pcmt.gg/players/otaka) entries. A thrifty saw [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) shift the momentum in the other direction, as their defensive coordination (as well as a 4k from [Haike](https://pcmt.gg/players/haike) and solid holds from [King](https://pcmt.gg/players/king)'s op) only let [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) win 1 more round, ending the half 7-5. [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) were able to convert the pistol on an A main push and an [Eva](https://pcmt.gg/players/eva) 3k, but the classics from [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) triggered a thrifty and another spiral of wins, as [Jamit](https://pcmt.gg/players/jamit) showed up consistently through every round, and [Pengu](https://pcmt.gg/players/pengu) chipping in with slight 2ks, pushing the score to a very quick 12-6. [Ghrf6](https://pcmt.gg/players/ghrf6) tried to claw back [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) into this game with 3 round wins in a row, but it was too little too late, and [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) took this fair 13-9, highlighted by [King](https://pcmt.gg/players/king) going 23/15/2

With the score tied 1-1, the ULTIMATE, INTENSE, MOST PRESSURE, DO-OR-DIE MAP 3 OF [ABYSS](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-11-01?map=Abyss) WAS NEXT. ALL OF THE PRESSURE, LOSER IS ELIMINATED... and only one team showed up to play.

[SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) converted the pistol and the eco, with the score starting 2-1 in their favor. From here on out, this map wasn't even close. [Nana](https://pcmt.gg/players/nana) was dropping multikills (and an ace!) like it was nothing, and it felt like the harbor/astra combo had no answers. There was some solidarity, with [Taylor](https://pcmt.gg/players/taylorgzz) giving [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) their map-ly round 3k, but it was too little, too late, as the attack half ended 10-2 for [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally). This time, there was nothing to go on for [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) in the 2nd half, as [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) put them down quickly winning the first three rounds and ending the afair 13-2, with a [Nana](https://pcmt.gg/players/nana) 4k at the end to put his statline at 20/5/3.

With this, [NOOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) is eliminated in 7th-8th place, and [SEAL](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) are set to face the loser of [NIP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/name-in-progress) vs [NATS](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/never-available-to-scrim).
