With the series being a best of five, both teams would only get one ban, with [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) opting to ban Haven, and [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) banning out Abyss. Heading into the map picks, [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) decided to start the series off on some comfortable ground, picking their stronghold of [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Summit). With their best map, Haven, banned, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) looked for their next best option, which they found in [Split](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Split), picking it for map two. [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)
 opted for [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Lotus) next, where they had seen some success with [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus)’s pocket pick Tejo. For map four, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) decided to gamble on their Sunset, which they had not won on heading into the series, which left Ascent as the final map in the series, which both teams had seen some success on.

The series began on [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s pick on [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Summit), where they showed why they were undefeated with a clean pistol win off the back of [Kaydeg](https://pcmt.gg/players/kaydeg)’s 3K, which he continued into the following round, picking up a 4K to cap off a 7/0 start. [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) clapped back with an anti-bonus conversion; however, the investment of the Phoenix ultimate spelled trouble for Round 4, which [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) would lose in a 2v3, forcing them onto a broken buy with a sheriff and a stinger, but [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) proved why he is the 2nd highest-rated player, bringing [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) back into the game with a 3K. The teams traded the next rounds back and forth, with some highlights being [KennyS](https://pcmt.gg/players/kennys)’s 1v1 clutch against [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) in round six and [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s thrifty in round eight. On round ten, [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) converted a 5v4 retake and, with [KennyS](https://pcmt.gg/players/kennys)’s help, put an end to the back-and-forth pattern, converting three rounds in a row to secure a 8-4 defensive half. After the swap, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) looked reinvigorated, claiming the pistol and the follow-up to bring the score to 6-8. But with [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) winning two of the next three rounds and [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) on an eco, the game was looking over. However, a huge three-man seize from [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) would allow [Ceiliuir](https://pcmt.gg/players/ceiliuir) to stick the spike, converting their eco despite four ultimates invested from [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence). This loss started [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s unraveling, losing five of the next seven rounds and putting the game in overtime off the back of a [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray) 1v1. Starting OT, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) converted a 2v3 to win their weak attack side, but were unable to close out the game due to a monster 1v2 from [Cassie](https://pcmt.gg/players/cassie) keeping [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) in the map. But with [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) converting two clean rounds to follow, it would be in vain as [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) closed out map one 15-13, starting off the series 1-0.

Heading into [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s pick of [Split](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Split), it was another story of early [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) dominance, starting off the half 6-3 despite two early rounds from [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1). [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray) started off [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s streak with a clean 3K on the anti-bonus, bringing the score to 1-2. [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) would only drop one of the next six rounds from here with some help from the voice comms curse, [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray)’s plays on the Omen and [KennyS](https://pcmt.gg/players/kennys)’s anchoring on Viper. With the help of a nice site execute, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) would stop the bleeding and pick up another two rounds with a tech pause in the middle, ending the half at 6-6. Starting off the second pistol, [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) looked to be in a bad position, ending up in a 3v5 postplant due to some well-placed spam from [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman), but heroics from [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray), [Cassie](https://pcmt.gg/players/cassie), and [KennyS](https://pcmt.gg/players/kennys) would allow them to convert it and take an early lead in the half, going up 8-7. The first full gun round of the half looked to be a close one, ending up in a 3v4 retake for [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) after a Phoenix ult from [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) ran through [Ceiliuir](https://pcmt.gg/players/ceiliuir)’s Cypher setup, but a well-timed push into CT backed up a Nightfall-spelled defeat for [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1), putting them back on the back foot. But with enough money for a buy, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) were able to clinch two more rounds, bringing the score to 9-9, where a hero rifle from [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) and some well-placed spam was enough to stop a [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) 1v3 and bring [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) the lead. Following the round, [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) would convert the next two rounds following a failed stinger rush and a well-played post-plant, bringing the score to 12-9, giving them three chances to close the map. Heading into round 22, both teams were equipped with full rifles, making it a very important round to decide the outcome. [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) opened the round posturing for a B split, but a double walk from [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) and [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) stopped it before it could come with a two-for-one trade. [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) would invest their Run it Back, but without enough space, [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) were able to cleanly retake despite the investment. Round 23 was another full gun round with a much similar opening look in a 0-2-3, with the two mid players finding the opening kill onto [Radical](https://pcmt.gg/players/radical), which led [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) to speed up into a Cypher setup on A, leading to [Cassie](https://pcmt.gg/players/cassie) getting picked off. Another kill in mid onto [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray) allowed T1 to secure a man advantage retake, which they handily converted, albeit with not much time left. The final round of regulation featured a broken buy from the side of [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence), who opted for a default and found the opening two kills. However, heroics from the remaining [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) players converted the round, only losing one more player and bringing the game to overtime. [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) tried to throw a curveball in the first round, but were swiftly dismantled by a well-timed flash from [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman), catching out three players in mid and all but securing the round, putting T1 on match point. The final round of the map saw [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) picked to pieces, with multiple players getting caught on timing and Roro securing a 3K to close out [Split](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Split) 14-12.

The final map of the series was [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s pick of [Lotus](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/t1-vs-navi-s3-playoffs-2026-10-24-2?map=Lotus), which saw [Linus](https://pcmt.gg/players/linus) bring out their pocket pick Tejo. However, in the first round this was not the standout, with [Sting](https://pcmt.gg/players/stingray)’s Chamber bringing [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) the pistol round. This was the only round of the first nine that they would win, with [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) steamrolling [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) to an early 8-1 lead, with [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) at the forefront and looking to break the kill record, dropping 101 across the series with 40 on the first map. Highlights from [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s eight-round streak include a successful round two stinger rush to start their dominance, a [MrCrocMan](https://pcmt.gg/players/mrcrocman) 1v3 on round four and a 3v5 on round seven to drop [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence)’s economy. On round 11, [NAVI](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/no-actual-valorant-intelligence) broke through [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s stranglehold on the map, scoring a much-needed round despite their worse economy and [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1)’s ultimate investments. They would continue this momentum for the rest of the half, turning a 1-8 into a 4-8. Going into the second half, a [Pi](https://pcmt.gg/players/pi) 3K on the pistol would stop this momentum as quickly as it came, bringing the score to 9-4. A stinger rush would prolong the inevitable, but [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) would win the following four rounds to close the map 13-6 and the series 3-0.

Up Next: [T1](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/team-1) takes on Group Omega second seed [SPG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s3-na/teams/spank-ptheory-gaming) in the Grand Finals, starting at 7:30 pm on Wednesday, Oct. 28.
