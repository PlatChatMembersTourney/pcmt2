The PCMT [EMEA Season 2](/events/s2-emea) [playoffs](/events/s2-emea?overview=Playoffs) opened with a reverse sweep as [Stay Seally](/events/s2-emea/teams/stay-seally) [beat](/events/s2-emea/faze-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-10-11) [Fabulous and Zesty](/events/s2-emea/teams/fabulous-and-zesty) 2-1 in Lower Round 1.

The series opened on SEAL's map pick of [Ascent](/events/s2-emea/faze-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-10-11?map=Ascent), a mainstay for both teams. FaZe started off strong, behind [thorny](/players/thorny)'s 1v3 pistol clutch. SEAL, not to be swayed, immediately answered back with a second round thrifty, which they converted into a 5-2 lead.

Those five rounds were the only rounds that SEAL would be able to find for the rest of the first half. Excellent site retakes and mutikills from the likes of [heef](/players/heef) and [Kramvi](/players/Kramvi) cemented FaZe's 7-5 lead going into the second.

The second pistol went the way of FaZe and this time, they were able to convert the follow up, extending their lead to 9-5. However, with SEAL's double Sentinel composition, the sites proved unassailable time and time again as the defenders stormed back to map point. With no room for error, FaZe called for a gutsy A execute, where a crucial [dragonflyod](/players/dragonflyod) 2K cracked open the site and forced overtime.

OT started FaZe on defense and as they'd shown in the first half, their retake ability proved the difference as they claimed map point. With sides switching, FaZe were finally able to put their attacking woes to bed as they claimed their opponent's map pick of Ascent 14-12.

[Lotus](/events/s2-emea/faze-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-10-11?map=Lotus), FaZe's map pick, started similarly to Ascent. FaZe secured the pistol, only to lose their anti-eco and the following two rounds. This time, FaZe were able to right the ship, conceding only two of the next eight rounds on the way to a 7-5 lead at the half.

The second half of Lotus showed us a different side to SEAL, winning six of the next ten rounds, 5 of which were retakes en route to a 12-10 scoreline. FaZe managed to scrape one round back before SEAL, off the back of [dovie](/players/dovie)'s 3K, converted another retake and sent our first playoff game to the decider.

The first half of [Sunset](/events/s2-emea/faze-vs-seal-s2-playoffs-2026-10-11?map=Sunset) was incredibly back and forth. FaZe continued to dominate the pistols but SEAL's Sage and Chamber comp continued to go round-for-round against FaZe's double Duelist.

The second half started with FaZe's fifth pistol of the series and looked like they were in the driver's seat after converting the bonus for an 8-6 lead. SEAL had other plans, turning up another gear as they managed to slip the net of FaZe's aggression and site stacks in what seemed like every round. A timeout proved fruitless for FaZe as SEAL raced away to a final scoreline of 13-8.

[heef](/players/heef) was the best player on the server in a losing effort: 304 ACS and 75 kills across the series and a series high 1.40 Rating<sup>T</sup>.

[Ghrf6](/players/ghrf6) solidified themself as one of the most consistent players in the server, putting up a team high 68 kills and a 1.24 Rating<sup>T</sup> on traditionally low-fragging agents, Sage and Viper.

Unfortunately, we have to say goodbye to our first team of the tournament. FaZe exit at the first playoff hurdle, but not without a fight.

## Up next

SEAL move on to Lower Round 2, where they'll face the loser of [XTC vs NOOT](/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-10-17).
