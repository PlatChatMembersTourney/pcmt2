The second weekend of PCMT [EMEA Season 2](/events/s2-emea) [playoffs](/events/s2-emea?overview=Playoffs) opened with [XTC](/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) melting the ground underneath [NOOT](/events/s2-emea/teams/penguin-patrol) in a [2-0](/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-10-17) fashion.

Due to an in-game bug effectively removing Haven from the competitive map pool, both teams agreed to replace it with Bind, which would have been the decider map of the series.

NOOT ended up leading the charge on their map pick of [Split](/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-10-17?map=Split) with a solid 3-0 start, converting the bonus round with a clean B execution, utilizing the strengths of their double duelist comp paired with [Jamit](/players/jamit)'s Skye flashes. However, a heroic Sheriff 2-piece from [Alex](/players/alex) on the 4th round ended up giving XTC a much-needed thrifty round.

The next round saw NOOT attempt an A split through vents, but [Riyako](/players/riyako) stood his ground with a 3k from ramps. This swung the momentum into XTC's favour for the rest of the half, meaning as the sides swapped, XTC were looking at a 7-5 lead.

The second half would prove perilous for NOOT, as they gave up a 15hp 1v2 clutch to [Zidin](/players/zidin) on the pistol. XTC converted the anti-eco, then won the bonus round when Alex's Vyse timed a lurk perfectly and caught the defenders off guard as they set up for the retake.

[Yessi](/players/yessi) single-handedly held off a 5-man flank on the 16th round, coming up with an ace at the best possible time for their team and XTC snowballed the rest of the map from there, not letting NOOT get their beaks into the game even for one round, ending Split with a 13-5 scoreline. Zidin was the unsung MVP for XTC, going 18/8/8 on the Omen, while [Taylor](/players/taylorgzz)'s Raze was the only one to hit double-digit kills (21/14/5) on the side of NOOT.

The second map was [Ascent](/events/s2-emea/noot-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-10-17?map=Ascent), XTC's map pick, where they unveiled a PCMT-special comp with [sinderinho](/players/sinderinho) locking in the 2nd Reyna pick of the season, after [Autumn](/players/autumn) did so on Haven against [NATS](/events/s2-emea/teams/never-available-to-scrim).

The opening pistol saw a 10-man brawl in mid, with NOOT's Jamit this time unable to salvage a 1v2 clutch as Riyako got another huge-impact 3k of the series to deny him. The anti-eco saw NOOT smell blood in the water as [Pengu](/players/pengu) got the entry on Yessi, who had been revealed by the Sova recon, but Sinderinho quickly stopped the retake with a stylish ranked-style Reyna 3k.

The first gun round for NOOT's defense already showed the cracks in their setup, as XTC completely took over the mid area of the map, splitting up NOOT's rotates and anchors, forcing them into 1v1s, which Riyako gladly took, winning the bonus round for his team and earning the title "Mr.3k" from the casters.

Nothing seemed to go NOOT's way for the next 8 rounds. XTC broke through their site holds and their retakes time and time again, getting the attackers to a staggering 11-0 lead, with rounds 10 and 11 coming from a 1v2 clutch by Sinderinho followed up by a 1v3 clutch by Riyako again to put what seemed a nail in NOOT's coffin.

An unsuccessful A rush from XTC saw NOOT finally get on the board in the last round of the half, but then XTC immediately won the next pistol round, setting the score at 12-1 in XTC's favour.

With all hope seemingly lost, NOOT forced a half-buy coming into round 14, and successfully overwhelmed XTC's B site defense with Taylor coming up with a massive multifrag. Slowly but surely, against all odds, NOOT dragged the game back to overtime, with Jamit finally coming alive on the Sova, winning a played-to-perfection 1v4 at 12-10 to keep NOOT afloat.

After the two teams combined for just 2 defense round wins in regulation, it was as if the world turned upside down in overtime, as neither of the teams could convert an attack round this time around, leading into a quintuple overtime in which XTC finally put an end to NOOT's comeback at 18-16.

Alex stole the show with a 1.29 rating and 52 kills across the series, while Jamit proved that a slow start doesn't mean a slow finish, going from 9 kills and 160 ACS on Split to an astounding 40 kills and 332 ACS on Ascent.

## Up next

As this PCMT EMEA Season 2 Playoffs weekend continues, we will see the second team be eliminated from the tournament in the game between [LARP](/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) and [HOT](/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) [tomorrow](/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18), Sunday 19/10/2026 at 20:00 CEST.
