The first round of the lower bracket of PCMT [EMEA Season 2](/events/s2-emea) [playoffs](/events/s2-emea?overview=Playoffs) came to a close with a blink-and-you’ll-miss-it 2-0 victory for [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) against [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan).

Due to an in-game bug effectively removing Haven from the competitive map pool, both teams agreed to replace it with Corrode. The map veto saw [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) pick [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Abyss) and [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) pick [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Corrode), with Lotus being the deciding map.

Finally being able to field their full roster, [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) immediately hit the ground running on [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Abyss), with [Duck’s](https://pcmt.gg/players/duck) clean entry snowballing a well-executed B split. Round 2 saw [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) commit mass s*icide jumping off the map to deny [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) any ultimate orbs. 

This effort would, however, end up in vain, as [Shipty](https://pcmt.gg/players/shipty) aced to give [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) the bonus round conversion. And from that point onwards, all eyes were glued to [Shipty](https://pcmt.gg/players/shipty), as he kept tearing [LARP's](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) shaky defensive setups apart, looking hungry to earn his team the win that qualified them in the group stage. [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Abyss) would be over as soon as it started, becoming the first 13-0 map in the history of [PCMT EMEA](/events/s2-emea). [Shipty](https://pcmt.gg/players/shipty) ended the map with the staggering scoreline of 27/4/0 and 538 ACS, just 2 kills shy of equaling the entire kill total of [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan).

Map 2 of [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Corrode) saw a much more competitive start, with [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) finally able to win a round in this series on the pistol, coming from some great team fighting in mid, with [Ellie](https://pcmt.gg/players/ellie) being able to close the round with a 2k to hold off the retake. But as soon as [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) began to believe, [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) snatched the hope away from them with a 2nd round thrifty culminating in a sneaky 1v1 stab in the back from [Tonnoman](https://pcmt.gg/players/tonnoman). The next 2 rounds went the way of [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) as well, with [Ducky](https://pcmt.gg/players/duck) on the Jett picking up some crucial kills to save his team in disadvantaged spots. A half buy A split attempt from [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) on round 5 reaped great rewards as the operator of [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures)  whiffed a few shots on the charging stingers, giving [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) a way back into the half. 

[LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) then went on to win 5 out of the next 7 rounds, with the highlights being Kaiser getting a clinical 1v2 in a postplant on low HP in round 7, and [oddlqt](https://pcmt.gg/players/oddlqt) closing it out in round 12 with a massive 4k in a clutch situation. The score at the half was 7-5 for [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan), and all seemed well, but then their defensive woes from [Abyss](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Abyss) resurfaced yet again. Not a single defensive round won on [Corrode](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/larp-vs-hot-s2-playoffs-2026-10-18?map=Corrode) saw [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) lose the map and the series with a 7-13 scoreline and a 0-2 map record. [Ducky](https://pcmt.gg/players/duck) was the star of the show on Corrode, posting a 21/10/2 scoreline with 277 ACS, a great sign for [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) as they welcome him back to play after he missed most of their group stage games due to scheduling issues.

Following this game's conclusion, we unfortunately have to say goodbye to [LARP](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/larpiatan) as they join [FaZe](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/fabulous-and-zesty) and exit PCMT [EMEA Season 2](/events/s2-emea) in a disappointing 9th-10th place. [HOT](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/honestly-outrageous-temperatures) advance to the lower bracket round 2, where they will face the loser of the game between [ATLC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/atlntic-esports) and [NATS](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/never-available-to-scrim).
