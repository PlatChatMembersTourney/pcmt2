This upper semi-finals matchup of [PCMT EMEA S2 Playoffs](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea?overview=Playoffs) was one to behold, as we have fan favorite [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog), finally playing after needing to wait a comically long amount of time, and [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed), a team who has looked incredibly hot and has been taking no prisoners. Going into this game, [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) has a new player, [K9](https://pcmt.gg/players/k-9), rostered, as Autumn did some no-no things :( On the side of [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed), they look to prove that their players' growth was not just for show, as [Riyako](https://pcmt.gg/players/riyako) proving that going from gold to diamond is not a fluke, and [sinder](https://pcmt.gg/players/sinderinho) looks to get off the bottom of the leaderboard.

The first map of this series took us to [Summit](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/sog-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-11-15?map=Summit), with some usual comps on both sides, with [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) running Chamber/Sage and [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) opting for Jett/Phoenix. [K9](https://pcmt.gg/players/k-9) exploded out of the gates with a 2k from B main, giving [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) an easy pistol win. The bonus would not be so easy, as [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) came out swinging with classics, and a 3k from [Riyako](https://pcmt.gg/players/riyako) made the score 1-1.

Even though the first thrifty was enough to get this game started, [faye](https://pcmt.gg/players/faye) had other ideas, collecting a 3k of her own with the classic and bringing the score back to a favorable 2-1. She continued this hot streak, having multikills on almost every round, as this first half was a back-and-forth affair between [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) and [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog), with the score reading 4-4 towards the end of the half. This is where [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) started to gain tons of momentum, as [sinder](https://pcmt.gg/players/sinderinho) FINALLY (and I mean finally) showed up, providing us with a couple multikills, and with the support from [alex](https://pcmt.gg/players/alex), closed out the half 8-4.

[SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) tried their best to mount a comeback during the attack half, but an early burst of 4 rounds from [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed), including multikills from [Riyako](https://pcmt.gg/players/riyako) and [sinder](https://pcmt.gg/players/sinderinho), pushed this game past comeback point. [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) ended on a 3-round win streak, but it was too little and too late, as the scoreline ended 13-10 in favor of [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed). This map was highlighted by incredible play from [faye](https://pcmt.gg/players/faye) (27/16/5), despite falling short of the victory.

The second map of this series was [Sunset](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/sog-vs-xtc-s2-playoffs-2026-11-15?map=Sunset), and to say this map was an email would be doing it too much. We saw a swap of roles from [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog)’s side, with [K9](https://pcmt.gg/players/k-9) on the duelist and [faye](https://pcmt.gg/players/faye) on the initiator, but [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) came out of the gate with a chip on their shoulder. Almost every single player on [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) was fragging out, and nothing was working for [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog). Retakes, site takes, defaults, nothing, as [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) completely obliterated them 13-1. This was highlighted by [Riyako](https://pcmt.gg/players/riyako) (16/2/7) and [Yessi](https://pcmt.gg/players/yessi) (16/3/4) going nuclear.

This series knocks [SOG](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/the-sog) down into the lower bracket to face the winner of HOT vs ATLC, and this pushes [XTC](https://pcmt.gg/events/s2-emea/teams/xually-terribly-cursed) into the upper bracket finals to face NIP.
